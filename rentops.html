RentOps

The operating workflow of a rental business, in one product.

Personal product — product direction and engineering led by Ans Khan.

Laravel 12 · Vue 3 · TypeScript · MySQL · Redis/Horizon · React Native

PurposeWorkflowsArchitectureInterfaceEvidenceStatus

Purpose and my role

Rental teams need to know what is available, who has each vehicle, what evidence exists and what action comes next. RentOps connects those decisions through a multi-tenant operations platform.

I lead the requirements, workflow design and implementation, using AI tools to support exploration and iteration while retaining responsibility for architecture, review and verification.

Operational workflows

Booking through settlement

Enquiry and quotation, pricing, availability and holds, reservation prerequisites, allocation, preparation, handover, extensions, replacements, return inspection and settlement.

Fleet and people

Vehicle documents and ownership, preventive maintenance and work orders, damage/insurance/dispute cases, chauffeur shifts and conflict-aware scheduling.

Financial operations

Invoices, payments, deposits, refunds, owner revenue rules and payouts, corporate contracts and billing, branch cash, currency-aware reporting and accounting exports.

Customer and platform surfaces

Public booking and check-in, communications and reminders, platform onboarding, tenant settings, permissions and approvals, integration and automation consoles.

Architecture and data integrity

A Laravel modular monolith owns the business rules, MySQL records and versioned APIs. Tenant/branch authorization extends into jobs, reports, storage and exports. Transactions, idempotency, version checks and guarded transitions protect allocation and financial state. Evidence records preserve metadata and integrity; queues and auditable recovery paths handle external work.

Interface design

I refined the interface around real operational tasks: availability planning, reservation detail, handover/return evidence, finance and owner settlements. Shared headers, searchable selectors, collapsible workspaces, clear action hierarchy, responsive navigation and useful empty/conflict states make the modules work as one product.

Mobile and automation

A separate React Native/TypeScript field client uses the same backend contracts for handover evidence, camera/GPS/signature capture, offline commands, resumable uploads, conflict review and revoke-and-wipe sign-out. Scale workflows include telematics ingestion, geofence alerts, advisory recommendations, governed pricing, country packs and white-label publishing.

Verification and measured evidence

The recorded 6 September 2026 engineering audit reports 129 backend tests / 1,185 assertions, 39 frontend tests and 32 browser scenarios across desktop/mobile engines. It covers tenant authorization, workflow transitions, finance, evidence, mobile sync and operational readiness. These are historical checkpoint results, not a fresh full-suite run.

Release status

Engineering implementation and local verification are documented. Production hosting rehearsal, independent security review, representative UAT and mobile physical-device/distribution approval remain external release gates. Integration adapters and configuration are described separately from live provider activation.
